# Commerce Infrastructure

> Ecommerce platform diligence, post-close stabilization, and fractional technical leadership for private-equity portfolio companies and holdcos.

Commerce Infrastructure helps investors and operators underwrite ecommerce platform risk before close and make the first 90 days after close boring.

## Services

### Pre-close diligence

A 10-business-day assessment of platform risk, operating cost, and an execution-ready 30/60/90-day plan.

### Post-close stabilization

A 90-day engagement focused on reliability, release safety, observability, and cost control.

### Technical leadership

Fractional platform leadership for portfolio operators who need experienced ecommerce technology direction.

## About

Commerce Infrastructure is a software development company based in Media, Pennsylvania, founded by Jason Hill, former CTO of Workarea Commerce (acquired in 2021). We bring two decades of experience building and operating technology platforms at scale.
